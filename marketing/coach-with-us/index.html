Coach With Us

Get Up and Flow adds coaches selectively as our client base grows. Our coaches work with dedicated clients through the whole workday — morning Jump Starts, check-ins and end-of-day reviews, Monday through Friday — supported by our proprietary training program, purpose-built tools and continuous quality review by our clinical team, led by Bruce Parsons, MD, PhD.

What we look for

  • A graduate-level degree in clinical psychology, or equivalent experience
  • Clinical or coaching experience — work with ADHD and executive function is a strong plus
  • Reliable weekday availability and excellent written and verbal communication
  • Warmth, structure and zero judgment
