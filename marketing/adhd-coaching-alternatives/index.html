Traditional one-on-one coaching

The deepest relationship and the most personalization, at the highest unit price: $75–$250 an hour, typically one session a week (full rate breakdown). Best if you want a senior credentialed coach and mainly need strategy, not daily follow-through.

Subscription coaching services

Services like Shimmer package coaching at a lower monthly price — about $230/month for a weekly 30-minute video session with text between sessions. A real coach relationship at a friendlier price; the unit is still one short session a week, so most of your week happens between touchpoints.

Body-doubling and coworking apps

Focusmate pairs you with a stranger for timed video focus sessions — three free a week, unlimited for about $8/month; group platforms run $20–$40. Excellent for powering through a block of work you’ve already chosen (how body doubling works). The limit: sessions are with strangers, on your initiative, session by session — no one plans the day with you or follows up on it.

Productivity apps alone

Cheap and useful for capture, hopeless at accountability: an app can’t notice avoidance or care if you show up. If apps were enough, you would not be reading this page.

All-day accountability coaching

Get Up and Flow gives you the same trained coach on call 8am–5pm every workday — morning planning, reminders at your commitment points, live help when you stall (a full day, hour by hour). Full Support is $750/month; Focus Lite is $200/month. On coverage, $750 buys roughly 195 coach-available hours a month — under $4 per covered hour versus $75–$250 for hourly coaching. Start tomorrow morning.

How to choose

Need strategy from a senior expert — traditional coach. Want a weekly coaching relationship on a budget — a subscription service. Already know what to do and just need company doing it — body doubling apps. Days keep collapsing between weekly sessions — that is the gap all-day coverage exists for.
