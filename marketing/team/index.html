Meet Our Leadership Team

Get Up and Flow was founded by a technologist and a psychiatrist who spent a year building one thing together: a practical, clinically informed way to help adults with ADHD get through their workdays. Hear it from us directly, then read more below.

Brian Fischman

Co-founder & CEO

brian@getupandflow.co

Brian is a technical founder and product leader who has spent his career building software systems and leading AI and product teams. At Get Up and Flow he leads the company and the technology: the proprietary platform, methodologies, and coach-supporting tools that make all-day accountability coaching possible. He co-developed the coaching curriculum with Dr. Parsons over a year of research and refinement.

Bruce Parsons, MD, PhD

Co-founder · Clinical Lead

bruce@getupandflow.co

Dr. Parsons is a board-certified psychiatrist and physician-scientist: a Rockefeller University PhD, Columbia-trained psychiatrist, former Assistant Professor at Columbia University College of Physicians and Surgeons where he led a neuropharmacology research laboratory, and a senior medical leader at Pfizer for nearly two decades across psychiatry, neurology, neuroscience, and pain. He is the author of more than 100 peer-reviewed publications and oversees the entire clinical side of Get Up and Flow.
