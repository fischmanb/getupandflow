Meet Our Leadership Team

Get Up and Flow was founded by a technologist and a psychiatrist who spent a year building one thing together: a practical, clinically informed way to help adults with ADHD get through their workdays.

Brian Fischman

CEO

brian@getupandflow.co

Brian is a technologist and product leader who has spent his career building software systems and leading AI and product teams. At Get Up and Flow he leads the company and the technology: the proprietary platform, methodologies and coach-supporting tools that make all-day accountability coaching possible. Along with Dr. Parsons’ strategic and clinical inputs, he developed the coaching curriculum over a year of research and refinement.

Bruce Parsons, MD, PhD

Chief Clinical Officer

bruce@getupandflow.co

Dr. Parsons is a board-certified psychiatrist and physician-scientist: a Rockefeller University PhD, Columbia-trained psychiatrist, former Assistant Professor at Columbia University College of Physicians and Surgeons where he led a neuropharmacology research laboratory. At Pfizer he served for two decades as the Global Medical Lead for Psychiatry, Neurology, Neuroscience and Pain. He is the author of more than 100 peer-reviewed publications and oversees the entire clinical side of Get Up and Flow.
