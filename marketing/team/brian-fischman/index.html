Brian Fischman

Co-founder & CEO, Get Up and Flow

brian@getupandflow.co

Brian Fischman is a technologist, product leader, and repeat founder who has spent more than twelve years building AI and machine learning products — from zero-to-one startups to some of the largest e-commerce and financial platforms in the world. He has also lived the problem Get Up and Flow exists to solve: diagnosed with ADHD in the 4th grade, he spent decades engineering his own systems, structures, and accountability to build a demanding career on top of a brain that doesn’t run on willpower alone. Get Up and Flow is the support system he wishes had existed the whole time.

Get Up and Flow began as Brian’s own experiment. He designed the business, the offerings, and the first draft of the coaching curriculum himself, then ran the proof of concept on the one client he knew best: himself, with a dedicated full-time coach at his back through every workday. Dr. Parsons joined a few months later, immediately taken with the concept, and together they spent the following year turning that prototype into the clinically grounded program Get Up and Flow runs today.

Brian co-founded his first venture-backed startup in 2013 as Chief Product Officer, growing subscription revenue more than 50% year over year for three consecutive years. At Walmart E-Commerce he led AI-driven personalization as Group Product Manager, among many responsibilities: managing a team of product managers, designing an orchestration layer that became core to Walmart’s omni-channel infrastructure, and created a $4B+ revenue expansion initiative. Earlier, he built ML-driven financial products at American Express and the first machine-learning recommendation system at Total Wine & More, which reached #1 US mobile market share by revenue in its category.

Brian went on to co-found Ascendia AI and lead it as CEO: a speech-based mental health diagnostics startup applying deep learning to 55+ vocal biomarkers across 18,000+ hours of clinical audio and 5,200 patients, backed by pre-seed investors including early backers of 23andMe and a multinational pharmaceutical company. On his own time, Brian designs cognitive AI infrastructure and knowledge-graph systems.

At Get Up and Flow, Brian leads the company and the technology: the proprietary platform, methodologies, and coach-supporting tools that make all-day accountability coaching possible, and the coaching curriculum he co-developed with Dr. Parsons over a year of research and refinement. He is a graduate of Columbia University, magna cum laude, where he skippered two seasons of the sailing team; a founding member of AI Circle, a community of leaders building the frontier of AI; and has logged more than 240 hours of healthcare volunteering with God’s Love We Deliver. He is a dedicated father and a Labrador Retriever enthusiast.
