The Daily Flow

(Full Support)

30 MIN LIVE

Jump Start: 30 Minutes

Start the day on a call with your coach — the same person as yesterday. Together you turn the calendar into color-coded blocks, set the day’s priorities, and leave with a plan that actually fits the day ahead.

Timely Reminders

Ahead of what’s next, your coach messages you the context you’ll want in your head — the point of the meeting, the state of the task, the files you’ll need — and walks the transition with you so nothing gets lost between blocks.

2×15 MIN LIVE

Bonus Time

Call Bonus Time and you get your coach live—up to 2 fifteen minute blocks of time to untangle the problem, shrink the task and sit with you while you start.

A Gentle Nudge

A light touch at the commitment points you chose in the morning — just enough to reopen the loop without breaking whatever focus you’ve got going.

Final Reminders

As the day winds down, your coach helps you close the open loops — the send, the save, the small thing tomorrow — you will be glad you finished.

15 MIN LIVE

Optional 15 Minute Retro

A short review with your coach: what worked, what got in the way and what tomorrow’s plan inherits. Most clients do it daily around day’s end — weekly or monthly works too.

Up to 75 minutes of live, face-to-face coach time available every day.
